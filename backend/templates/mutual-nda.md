### 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1 **Scope.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public, confidential, or proprietary information disclosed by or on behalf of one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other Party ("Receiving Party"), whether disclosed verbally, visually, electronically, or in tangible documentary or digital form, that:  
(a) is marked, designated, or stamped with a restrictive legend such as "Confidential", "Proprietary", or words of similar import; or  
(b) given the nature of the information disclosed or the circumstances surrounding its disclosure, reasonably should be recognized and understood by a prudent commercial entity to be confidential. Confidential Information specifically includes, without limitation, source code, system architectures, trade secrets, research methodologies, customer directories, commercial pricing models, business roadmaps, and security specifications.

1.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information shall not include any information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate through verifiable contemporaneous documentary evidence:  
(a) is or becomes publicly known and generally available through no act, omission, or breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;  
(b) was already rightfully in the possession of the Receiving Party without confidentiality restrictions prior to the disclosure by Disclosing Party;  
(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party's personnel or contractors without access to, reference to, or reliance upon Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or  
(d) is rightfully obtained by the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis from an independent third party who was not under an obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party.

---

### 2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 **Standard of Care.** Receiving Party shall protect and preserve the confidentiality of Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like nature and importance, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

2.2 **Permitted Use.** Receiving Party shall access, inspect, and use Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely and exclusively to evaluate and execute the Authorized Purpose, and for no other purpose whatsoever. Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, copy, reproduce, or exploit any Confidential Information for any commercial, competitive, or independent benefit.

2.3 **Need-to-Know Dissemination.** Receiving Party may disclose Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to its authorized directors, officers, employees, legal counsel, accountants, and verified technical consultants ("Representatives") who:  
(a) have a bona fide operational need to know such information in direct furtherance of the Authorized Purpose; and  
(b) are bound by written confidentiality agreements or statutory professional obligations at least as restrictive as those contained in this Agreement. Receiving Party remains fully responsible and liable for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives.

---

### 3. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE & SUBPOENAS

If Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required or compelled by applicable law, regulation, valid subpoena, court order, or governmental inquiry to disclose any Confidential Information of Disclosing Party, Receiving Party shall:  
(a) provide Disclosing Party with prompt written notice (to the extent legally permissible) before making such disclosure so that Disclosing Party may seek a protective order, confidential treatment, or other appropriate legal remedy; and  
(b) furnish only that portion of the Confidential Information that is legally mandated to be disclosed, exercising all reasonable efforts to ensure that confidential treatment will be accorded to the disclosed information.

---

### 4. RETURN OR CERTIFIED DESTRUCTION OF MATERIALS

4.1 **Return Obligation.** Upon written request by Disclosing Party or upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Receiving Party shall promptly, and in any event within fourteen (14) calendar days:  
(a) cease all use of Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; and  
(b) return or permanently erase, purge, and destroy all documents, notes, analyses, storage media, and electronic files embodying Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

4.2 **Certification.** Upon request, an authorized officer of Receiving Party shall certify in writing that all materials containing Confidential Information have been returned or destroyed in accordance with this Section. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Receiving Party may retain one archival copy solely for statutory compliance and audit record-keeping, subject to continuing confidentiality obligations.

---

### 5. EQUITABLE RELIEF AND REMEDIES

The Parties acknowledge and agree that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement may cause immediate and irreparable harm to Disclosing Party for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Accordingly, in addition to any other legal or financial remedies available under law or equity, Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek temporary, preliminary, and permanent injunctive relief and specific performance against any actual or threatened breach without the necessity of posting any bond or demonstrating special damages.

---
