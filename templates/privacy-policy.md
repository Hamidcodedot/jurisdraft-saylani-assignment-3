### 1. SCOPE & COMMITMENT TO DATA PRIVACY

{{company_legal_name}} ("we", "us", or "our") operates {{website_url}} and provides automated legal document drafting, management, and repository solutions. We are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of the personal data entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, disclose, and secure your personal information in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international data protection frameworks.

### 2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED

2.1 **Information Provided Directly by You:**  
- **Identity & Contact Data:** Full name, corporate entity, job title, email address, physical address, and telephone number.  
- **Account & Credential Data:** Encrypted passwords, authentication tokens, and account profile preferences.  
- **Agreement & Transactional Data:** Document counterparty names, terms, jurisdiction selections, custom clauses, and optional digital signatures.

2.2 **Information Collected Automatically:**  
- **Device & Network Telemetry:** IP address, browser type, operating system version, and system language.  
- **Usage & Interaction Logs:** Feature navigation timestamps, API request latency, error telemetry, and session durations.

### 3. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING (GDPR ARTICLE 6)

We process personal data only when lawful legal bases exist under applicable statutory law:  
(a) **Performance of Contract:** To provision user accounts, compile legal agreements, synchronize vault storage, and process transactions.  
(b) **Legitimate Business Interests:** To maintain platform uptime, detect and mitigate security threats, and prevent fraudulent abuse.  
(c) **Statutory Legal Obligations:** To comply with financial accounting, taxation, anti-fraud, and corporate governance laws.  
(d) **Consent:** Where you have granted explicit consent for specific informational communications.

---

### 4. ABSOLUTE ZERO AI MODEL TRAINING GUARANTEE

We maintain an uncompromising privacy invariant: **We never use, share, or disclose customer legal documents, agreement clauses, party names, or vault data to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence models.** All AI inference pipelines operate strictly stateless, and customer document prompts are immediately purged from volatile memory upon response generation.

---

### 5. THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE & SUB-PROCESSORS

We never sell, monetize, or rent your personal information to third parties. We disclose data solely to vetted enterprise sub-processors under signed Data Protection Addenda (DPA) enforcing statutory confidentiality:  
- **Cloud Infrastructure Providers:** Secure hosting and multi-tenant database services.  
- **Payment Processors:** PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways.  
- **Transactional Communications:** Automated email dispatchers for account verification.

### 8. YOUR STATUTORY PRIVACY RIGHTS

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar data privacy regulations, you possess enforceable rights regarding your personal information:  
- **Right of Access:** Request copies of personal data held regarding you.  
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated data.  
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your personal records and vault documents.  
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.  
- **Right to Object & Restrict:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests or automated profiling.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Desk at {{privacy_contact_email}}. We respond to verifiable inquiries within thirty (30) calendar days without charge.

---

### 9. ENTERPRISE SECURITY MEASURES

We enforce rigorous defense-in-depth security controls:  
- **Encryption in Transit:** All communications are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).  
- **Encryption at Rest:** Sensitive databases and document records are encrypted using AES-256.  
- **Access Controls:** Strict least-privilege role-based access control (RBAC) and automated intrusion logging.
